Sheet cutting optimizer NEW · v3

Pack saved drawer panels onto stock sheets. Layouts are estimates for planning and should be checked before cutting.

Save at least one drawer to generate a cutting layout.

Planning limitations: Pieces are grouped into separate stock sheets by material thickness, even if “All” is selected. The packing algorithm is a rectangle heuristic—not a guarantee of minimum sheets or a saw-ready guillotine cutting sequence. Saw kerf is reserved between adjacent placements, edge trim is excluded from usable area, but no additional crosscut/hold-down allowances are calculated. Rotating pieces is inappropriate for directional wood grain, veneers or patterned laminates; disable rotation in those cases. Dimensions are in mm regardless of the main calculator display units. Verify stock dimensions, grain, hardware and actual cuts before manufacturing.
