Sheet cutting optimizer NEW · v3

Pack saved drawer panels onto stock sheets. Layouts are estimates for planning and should be checked before cutting.

Save at least one drawer to generate a cutting layout.

Planning limitations: Pieces are grouped into separate stock sheets by material thickness, even if “All” is selected. The packing algorithm is a rectangle heuristic—not a guarantee of minimum sheets or a saw-ready guillotine cutting sequence. Saw kerf is reserved between adjacent placements, edge trim is excluded from usable area, but no additional crosscut/hold-down allowances are calculated. Rotating pieces is inappropriate for directional wood grain, veneers or patterned laminates; disable rotation in those cases. Dimensions are in mm regardless of the main calculator display units. Verify stock dimensions, grain, hardware and actual cuts before manufacturing.

These are independent fabrication worksheets using your current drawer dimensions. They do not replace the standard butt-joint cut list or automatically change the sheet optimizer. Convert checked dimensions into a saved cut list only after confirming the construction and hardware.

Captured bottom in continuous grooves in all four walls. The floor panel enters the grooves; wall blanks remain sized to the basic box geometry, assuming butt joints.

Design check: consider assembly order (a captive panel often has to slide in before closing the fourth wall). Grooves must leave adequate material below them, avoid conflicts with joinery, and suit load and wood movement. Router bit size and fit must be verified on scrap.

Through-dovetail blank sizes and tail spacing worksheet. This does not design the final pin/socket geometry or a machine-ready toolpath.

Joint orientation matters: through-dovetail front/back blanks usually span the outside box width, unlike the app's butt-joint front/back cut list. Do not use both cut lists interchangeably. Tail pitch is a layout guide, not a full joint template.

No universal Blum / Hettich / Häfele geometry exists. Enter the exact manufacturer installation-sheet dimensions for your model and revision. This is an annotation worksheet, not a validated drilling template.

Never drill or notch using assumed generic offsets. Some slide systems require particular side thickness, inside depth, locking-device positions, rear panel elevation or a specific bottom recess. Confirm every dimension against the exact manufacturer manual.

Single drawer decorative front, aligned to the cabinet opening (not the drawer box). Inset fronts use reveal gaps; overlay fronts extend beyond the opening.

For a bank of drawers, cumulative reveals, overlays and neighboring front clearances must be designed together. These calculations assume one rectangular opening, a square front, and a centered horizontal two-hole pull.
